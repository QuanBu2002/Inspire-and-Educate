Who We Are

Three sisters. One mission.

Our Origin Story

It started with a whisper. During community service, three sisters noticed something the food and clothing drives weren't reaching.

In 2024, Alexandra, Olivia, and Madelyne Dongnguyen were volunteering at a local community service event. Women came for food. They came for clothing. But some of them quietly pulled the sisters aside to ask for something else — sanitary supplies.

That moment changed everything. The sisters realized that menstrual products — items most of us take for granted — were a silent and unmet need for thousands of women across the Bay Area. The mainstream food and clothing drives weren't designed to provide them. The shame around asking made the problem invisible.

So they founded Inspire and Educate. Today, the organization works to provide equal access to menstrual health products to vulnerable populations across the Bay Area, raise awareness of period poverty, and improve quality of life for those in need.

Our Mission

To provide equal access to menstrual health products, raise awareness of period poverty, and improve the quality of life for those in need.

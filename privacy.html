Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Inspire & Educate respects the privacy of donors, volunteers, partners, and visitors. This policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how to contact us with questions.

Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, email, volunteer communications, product drive coordination, or donation links. This may include your name, email address, message, interest area, donation information, and related contact details.

Donations

Online donations are processed through Givebutter. Payment information is handled by Givebutter and its payment-processing partners. Inspire & Educate does not store full payment card numbers on this website.

How we use information

  • Respond to questions, volunteer inquiries, and partnership requests.
  • Coordinate product drives, donation logistics, and community outreach.
  • Send donation receipts or campaign updates when applicable.
  • Improve the website and the organization’s programs.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the website, process donations, manage forms, or support organizational communications.

Website analytics and forms

The website may use hosting, form, and basic analytics services that process technical information such as browser type, device information, referrer, and pages visited.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to inspireandedu@gmail.com.

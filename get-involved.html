03

Host a Drive

Rally your school, office, or community group. Product drives are one of the best ways to get supplies to us in real volume.

Anyone can run a drive.

Run a drive at your school, your office, your faith community, or your friend group. We provide the materials, guidance, and pickup logistics. You bring the people.

Most-needed items: pads (any size), tampons (any absorbency), liners, menstrual cups, and underwear.

Start a Drive →
  • 1Tell us about your drive. Send us details about your group, location, and timeline.
  • 2Get the toolkit. We send posters, social graphics, FAQ sheets, and collection bins.
  • 3Run the drive. Typical drives last 2–4 weeks. We're available throughout for support.
  • 4We handle pickup. When you're done, we collect, sort, and distribute the products.

03

Host a Drive

Rally your school, office, faith community, or neighborhood group to collect menstrual products.

Anyone can run a drive.

Run a drive at your school, office, community group, or friend group. We can help with guidance and pickup coordination.

Most-needed items include pads, tampons, liners, menstrual cups, and underwear.

Start a Drive →
  • 1. Tell us about your drive. Send details about your group, location, and timeline.
  • 2. Get the toolkit. We help with suggested materials and logistics.
  • 3. Run the drive. Collect products over a defined period.
  • 4. Coordinate delivery. We help move products toward distribution.

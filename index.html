Why It Matters

What changes when she has what she needs.

Access to menstrual products lets people go to school, hold a job, and move through the world without interruption. Without them, that gets a lot harder.

A period should never be the reason someone misses school, skips work, or stays home.
  • 01

    Lost Independence

    Without menstrual products, women cannot leave home. They lose mobility, miss work, and are unable to participate in society for days every month.

  • 02

    Missed Education

    Affected youth miss school frequently, fall behind in their classwork, and feel isolated from their peers. Educational inequality compounds.

  • 03

    Serious Health Risks

    Many women without hygiene products use the same tampon for dangerously long periods, putting them at risk of serious infection and toxic shock.

  • 04

    Loss of Dignity

    Period poverty carries a profound emotional weight. Shame, anxiety, and a sense of invisibility compound the physical hardship.
